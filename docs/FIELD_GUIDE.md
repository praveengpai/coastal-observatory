# Field guide: setting up a phone and using the forms

For ASHAs, volunteers, One Health volunteers, the MSW and the NIO team. Print one page per person.
Malayalam text in the forms is a machine-assisted translation for accessibility; English is the reference.

## Set up the phone once (coordinator, 10 minutes)

1. Install **ODK Collect** from the Play Store.
2. Open it and scan the person's **App User QR code** from ODK Central. The forms appear by themselves.
3. Install **Gboard** if it is not the keyboard already. In Gboard settings, add **Malayalam**
   and choose the **Malayalam (Manglish)** layout as well. Turn on **Voice typing**.
4. In ODK Collect settings: turn on **Auto send: only with Wi-Fi** if data packs are short, or **Wi-Fi or data**.
5. Fill one test form with the site **Training / test site** and send it, to check everything works.

## At the camp

- The first form of the day: choose the **site** and your **role or station**. After that they fill
  in by themselves.
- **Scan, don't type**: scan the person sticker on the health card and the household sticker.
  If a sticker will not scan, choose "Type" and enter the code carefully.
- To speak instead of typing: tap the **microphone key** on the keyboard and speak in Malayalam or English.
- If the person does not live here permanently, answer **No** to "Lives here permanently?" and fill the
  visitor details. Everyone else skips them.
- No internet? Keep working. Forms are saved on the phone and **send themselves** when the phone
  reaches Wi-Fi or data. Do not delete ODK Collect or clear its data before forms are sent.
- Check "Ready to send" in ODK Collect at the end of the day. It should be zero once you are online.

## Between camps (ASHAs and volunteers)

- Use **Household visit** for every visit: scan the household sticker, record illness counts,
  referral follow-up, elder checks and the advice you gave. This closes your tasks on the dashboard.
- Use **Water sample** for every bottle: scan the bottle label, stand next to the source for GPS.

## Stickers

| Sticker | Looks like | Goes on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Person | OBS-P-000123 | The health card, given at the welcome desk |
| Household | CVB-014 | The household's card or door; same code for everyone in the house |
| Water sample | WS-000123 | The sample bottle |

Never reuse a sticker. Return unused sticker sheets to the coordinator.
