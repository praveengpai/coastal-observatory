Kochi Coastal One Health Observatory

Install the field app
ഫീൽഡ് ആപ്പ് ഇൻസ്റ്റാൾ ചെയ്യുക

For ASHAs, volunteers and the NIO team. Takes about 3 minutes. Needs an Android phone.

1

Install ODK Collect

ODK Collect ഇൻസ്റ്റാൾ ചെയ്യുക

Tap the button, then tap Install in the Play Store. It is free.

Get ODK Collect on Google Play
2

Scan your setup QR code

നിങ്ങളുടെ സെറ്റപ്പ് QR കോഡ് സ്കാൻ ചെയ്യുക

Open ODK Collect and choose Configure with QR code. Scan the code the coordinator shows you. Your forms download by themselves.

3

Fill a form

ഫോം പൂരിപ്പിക്കുക

Tap Start new form and pick the form. Choose your site once; the next forms remember it for the day.

For practice, choose the site TST (test site). Practice entries never mix with real data.

4

Sending is automatic

അയയ്ക്കൽ സ്വയം നടക്കും

No signal? Keep working. Saved forms send themselves when the phone finds mobile data or wifi. To check, open Send finalized form.

Typing made easy

  • Speak instead of typing: tap the microphone on the keyboard.
  • Malayalam or Manglish: in Gboard settings, add the Malayalam keyboard. Type in English letters and pick the Malayalam word.
  • IDs: scan the QR sticker instead of typing the number.

Keep your QR code private

Anyone with your setup code can send forms in your name. Do not post it in WhatsApp groups. If a phone is lost, tell the coordinator and the code will be switched off.

No Android phone, or stuck? Ask your coordinator. Malayalam text is a machine-assisted translation for accessibility; English is the reference.
